Chapter 5 - Looking up, Listening in

Better to be a fool and not open your mouth instead of open your mouth and prove it.

Let your heart not be hasty. let your words be few. 

"Hear oh israel" 

Real faith and trust in God are not compartmentialized

We know what its like to be fragmented. 

We feel dry. He seems distant. And a real relationship seems out of reach... 

What should we do? LISTEN. HEAR. Listen up Israel. 

We need to put God's words on our hearts. Lodged in our mental processes.

The ear is the Christians primary sense organ.

We want to speak more than we want to listen.

What do we see in human activity? 
    Suffering
    Lectures, opinions, driven by envy, waffling
    No one really knows why we do what we do. 

Speaking and knowing the world only gets us so far, but all is not lost. 

We may not be able to read the book of creation (the world) but we can read the book of the law (the bible).
Its a fallen world and we can't always fully interpret it.

Part 2: Speaking to God verses 4 -7
    - How should we speak to God? (making vows is odd)
    - When you tell God you will do something, then do it. its better to not make a vow in the first place.
    - God is in heaven and will bring to justice the righteous and the wicked.
    - God wants someone whos yes is yes and whos no is no.
    - Vows were permitted. But not needed. 
    - Vows, oaths, exist because we are untruthful. "I swear, this happened" - wrapped untruthfulness in a promise of truthfulness
    - The principle: When you speak to God and others, simplicity guards your sinerity
    - God knows if we are telling the Truth.
    - Is there more coming in than going out? 
    - The reality of God is measured by the truthfullness of his speach not by my grasp of his presence.
    - God speaks and the only correct response is that we will listen and obey.
